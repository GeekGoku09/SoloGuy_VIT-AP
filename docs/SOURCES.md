# Key Epidemiological & Clinical Sources

1. **ICMR-INDIAB Study (2023):**
   - **Details:** An estimated 101 million people in India have diabetes, and 136 million have prediabetes.
   - **Citation:** Anjana, R. M., et al. "Metabolic non-communicable disease burden in India: progress and challenges (ICMR-INDIAB study)." *The Lancet Diabetes & Endocrinology* 11.7 (2023): 474-489.
   - **Status:** Verified via ICMR-INDIAB published national survey results.

2. **Indian Food Composition Tables (IFCT 2017):**
   - **Details:** Nutritional profile and macronutrient composition of standard Indian dietary preparations.
   - **Citation:** Longvah, T., et al. "Indian Food Composition Tables." National Institute of Nutrition, Indian Council of Medical Research (2017).
   - **Status:** Verified via NIN ICMR standard food composition standards.
